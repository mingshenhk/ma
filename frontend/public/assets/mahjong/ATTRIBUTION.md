# Mahjong tile artwork

The face images in `faces/` are copied from the `tiles/` directory of [`zddd312/mahjong-tiles`](https://github.com/zddd312/mahjong-tiles), using its 1m–9m character tiles, 1p–9p circle tiles, 1s–9s bamboo tiles, and `z5` red dragon. The asset filenames here are renamed to match this app's tile values.

The upstream repository's [README](https://github.com/zddd312/mahjong-tiles#%E8%AE%B8%E5%8F%AF) says its license terms also cover the tile images and SVGs; the [license text](https://github.com/zddd312/mahjong-tiles/blob/main/LICENSE) permits use and redistribution with or without modification, for commercial or non-commercial use, without warranty. The upstream README credits `lietxia/mahjong_graphic` and I.Mahjong-HK / 內木一郎 as sources. Keep this notice with the assets and consult the upstream terms if redistributing them separately.
